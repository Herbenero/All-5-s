All 5's

Dart scoring app — race to 51

Setup Mode
pub rules scorer

Every round lives or dies by 5.

Add players, select your board, start the match, and score three darts per turn. A round only scores if the total is divisible by 5. Each complete 5 equals 1 point. First player to 51 wins. If the third dart is a miss, the round scores zero.

Three darts Each player throws exactly 3 darts per round. The app tracks every dart in order.
Divisible by 5 Total round score must divide cleanly by 5. Example: 15 total = 3 points.
Third dart miss If dart 3 is a miss, the round is worth 0 points, even if the running total looked valid.
